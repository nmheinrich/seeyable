Seeyable · Microphone setup

One-time, user-initiated permission step for voice commands in the side panel.

Chrome grants microphone access per extension origin. The side panel cannot always show that prompt itself, so this page asks once. The microphone stream is stopped immediately after the permission answer — no audio is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere by this page.

After granting access, close this tab, return to the page you want to operate, open the Seeyable side panel, and choose “Start listening”. Browser speech recognition may still process recognized audio outside this device; Seeyable itself stores no audio or transcripts.
